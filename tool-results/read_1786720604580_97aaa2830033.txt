    25→## 1. Method
    26→
    27→### 1.1 What "critical path" means here
    28→
    29→The critical path is the **longest chain of blocking B-edges** from a root (no B-dependency) to a leaf (no B-dependent). Any P0 on this chain, if delayed, delays every downstream P0 on the chain — and thus delays launch (because launch requires all P0s at `Production-ready`).
    30→
    31→**Path length** = number of B-edges in the chain (number of nodes minus one).
    32→
    33→### 1.2 Rules (locked from Artifact 2 + stakeholder boundary)
    34→
    35→1. **Only `--B[blocking]-->` edges** are path edges. `--B[non-blocking]-->` and `--B[parallelizable]-->` do not extend a path (the dependent can proceed without the dependency at `Production-ready`).
    36→2. **`--F-->` feature interactions** are super-blocking nodes — all member P0s must be `Production-ready` AND the interaction test must pass. An F-node attaches to the path as a join: the path cannot complete the interaction until all members are ready.
    37→3. **`--P-->` failure-propagation edges** are NEVER path edges. They weight risk/criticality only.
    38→4. **P0-27 (Deployment)** is an isolated control node — excluded from the business critical path. It must reach `Production-ready` before launch (it is on the launch gate), but it does not block other P0s.
    39→5. **Multiple co-critical paths are accepted.** If two or more paths share the maximum length, they are all critical. No forced compression into a single chain.
    40→
    41→### 1.3 Computation
    42→
    43→For each leaf, trace backward through blocking B-edges to roots. The longest such chain defines the critical path(s). Multiple leaves may share the maximum length → multiple co-critical paths.

   191→### 4.1 F-node classification framework
   192→
   193→| Class | Definition | Critical-path effect | Example |
   194→|-------|------------|----------------------|---------|
   195→| **Synergy** | Two features enhance each other but neither technically requires the other; can ship independently | Does NOT extend path length; NOT a synchronization gate | (none currently identified at P0 — most P0-relevant interactions are stronger) |

   215→### 4.3 F-node effect on critical path (corrected)
   216→
   217→- **Synergy:** No effect on path length or launch-readiness gating.
   218→- **Synchronization (incl. Security/Integrity sync):** Synchronization gate — extends *effective* launch-readiness (the terminus cannot be `Production-ready` until all members are ready + interaction test passes) but does NOT extend path *length* (it is a join, not a chain extension).
